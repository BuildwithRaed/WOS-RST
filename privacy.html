WOS RST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

WOS RST (www.wosrst.com) is a free, unofficial planner for the game Whiteout Survival. This page explains, in plain words, what the site saves about you, why, and how to have it deleted. In short: we only keep what the planner needs to work. We don't show ads, and we never sell or share your data.

What we save

  • Your planner data: what you enter on the site, such as levels, targets, Backpack amounts, heroes, purchases and pack changes, and the commander name if you set one. It is saved so the site remembers it next time.
  • A guest account: when you first open the site you get an anonymous guest account (just a random ID, no name or email), so your planner data can be saved without signing up.
  • Your account details, only if you create an account: your email address and your chosen language (so our emails come in your language). Your password is stored scrambled, so nobody, including us, can read it.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google tells us your email address and, depending on your Google settings, your name and profile picture. We use only the email to identify your account. We don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.
  • We don't show ads and don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Scan screenshot: game screenshots you scan are read on your own phone or computer and are never uploaded. Only the amounts you choose to save are kept.
  • Other visitors can't see your planner data or your email.

Stored in your browser

Your browser keeps your login, so you stay signed in, and a few settings such as language, currency and the last page you opened. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Services that help run the site

These services handle data only to make the site work:

  • Supabase: stores accounts and planner data, and handles sign-in.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Resend: sends account emails (confirm your email, reset your password).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr: delivers some of the site's program files.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: counts visits without cookies and without identifying you.

Like almost every website, these services can see your IP address when your browser connects to them.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all your saved planner data at any time. Email contact@wosrst.com from the email address of your account (or, for a guest account, tell us roughly what you saved) and we will delete it within 30 days.

To remove only what is in this browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Children

The site is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Email contact@wosrst.com.

WOS RST is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Century Games, the maker of Whiteout Survival.
